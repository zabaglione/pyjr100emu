STONE BALANCE

Take turns removing 1–3 stones from one of three piles over ten matches. In the first five, taking the last stone wins; in the last five, taking it loses.

3つの山から1〜3個ずつ取り合う全10局です。前半5局は最後の石を取った側が勝ち、後半5局は取った側が負けになります。

Controls and play

W/S selects a pile, A/D selects how many stones to take, and RETURN takes them. You cannot take from several piles in one turn. Your opponent also chooses winning moves when available, so plan the shape of the piles you leave behind.

Directions work on the keyboard or pad; the pad button also acts as RETURN. SPACE opens confirmation to restart this stage. NO is selected initially. Choose with A/D, confirm with RETURN, or cancel with SPACE. Play pauses during confirmation. CTRL+C returns to BASIC.

Stones sit on shelves with visible depth. Selected stones fly off the board one at a time. Your move and the opponent's move have separate sounds and pauses; the result appears after the last stone can be seen leaving. Wait for animations to finish before pressing again.

LAST STONE WINS / LOSES states this match's rule. TAKE is the chosen amount, and RIVAL TOOK is the opponent's previous amount. Arrows mark the stones you are about to take.

操作と遊び方

W/Sで山、A/Dで取る数を選び、RETURNで取ります。複数の山から同時には取れません。相手も勝てる手を選ぶので、残す山の形を考えます。

方向キーはキーボードまたはパッド、RETURNはパッドのボタンでも操作できます。SPACEでこの面のやり直し確認を開きます。やり直し確認はNOが初期選択です。A/Dで選び、RETURNで確定、SPACEで取り消します。確認中は進行を止めます。CTRL+CでBASICへ戻ります。

厚みのある棚に石を並べ、選んだ石が1個ずつ盤外へ飛びます。自分と相手の手を別の間と音で示し、最後の石を確認してから結果を出します。演出が終わってから次のキーを押してください。

LAST STONE WINS／LOSESが今回の勝敗条件、TAKEが取る数、RIVAL TOOKが相手の直前の個数。取る対象の石に矢印が付きます。
