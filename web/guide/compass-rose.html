COMPASS ROSE

Locate buried relics at ten sites using your last measured bearing and distance band. Readings do not update automatically when you move: choose survey locations that narrow the possibilities.

Controls and play

WASD moves, X surveys, and RETURN digs. Moving costs 1 supply and surveying costs 2. Your initial survey is free, with up to four additional surveys. You can miss twice; the third empty dig fails the attempt.

Directions work on the keyboard or pad; the pad button also acts as RETURN. SPACE opens confirmation to restart this stage. NO is selected initially. Choose with A/D, confirm with RETURN, or cancel with SPACE. Play pauses during confirmation. CTRL+C returns to BASIC.

A survey expands through three rings. Walking passes through intermediate positions; digging creates a hole, and discovery has light and sound. Previous survey locations remain marked. Wait for the animation to finish before pressing again.

BEARING shows N/S/W/E. NEAR means distance 0–2, MID 3–5, and FAR 6 or more. Distance is the sum of horizontal and vertical steps. FROM records the survey's column and row; SURVEYS is remaining surveys, STEPS is supply, and DIGS is digs available.

操作と遊び方

WASDで移動、Xで測定、RETURNで掘ります。移動は補給1、測定は2。開始時の測定は無料で、追加は4回までです。掘り外せるのは2回で、3回目の空振りで失敗します。

方向キーはキーボードまたはパッド、RETURNはパッドのボタンでも操作できます。SPACEでこの面のやり直し確認を開きます。やり直し確認はNOが初期選択です。A/Dで選び、RETURNで確定、SPACEで取り消します。確認中は進行を止めます。CTRL+CでBASICへ戻ります。

測定では輪が3段階に広がり、歩行は途中の位置を通ります。掘る動きと穴、発見時の光と音を表示し、過去の測定地点を残します。演出が終わってから次のキーを押してください。

BEARINGはN/S/W/Eの方角、NEARは距離0〜2、MIDは3〜5、FARは6以上。距離は上下左右の合計です。FROMは測定地点の列・行、SURVEYSは残り測定、STEPSは補給、DIGSは掘れる回数です。

Made for the JR-100 with standard 16 KB RAM. Emulator-tested; real hardware is unverified.標準RAM 16KBのJR-100向け。エミュレーターで検証済みです。実機での動作・音声は未確認です。
